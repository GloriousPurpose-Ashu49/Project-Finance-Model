**Project Finance Model – 200 MW Solar Power Project**

Project Overview:
Developed a financial model for a 200 MW solar power project to analyse its investment requirements, funding structure, cash flows and financial returns over the project life.

Project Details:
- Project Capacity: 200 MW
- Construction Period: January 2026 – December 2027
- Commercial Operation Date (COD): January 2028
- Project Life: 25 Years
- Total Project Cost: ₹759.9 Crore
- Funding Structure: 30% Equity and 70% Debt
- Expected Annual Power Generation: 473.04 Million Units

Key Findings:

1. **Project Investment:** The total estimated project cost is approximately ₹759.9 crore, with solar panels accounting for the major share of investment.

2. **Revenue Growth:** Annual revenue is projected to increase from ₹141.9 crore in 2028 to ₹267.6 crore in 2041.

3. **Profitability:** Profit after tax is expected to grow from ₹26.8 crore in 2028 to ₹130 crore in 2041.

4. **Debt Repayment:** The model includes a long-term debt repayment schedule, interest expenses and working capital requirements.

5. **Debt Servicing Capacity:** DSCR starts at 2.13x, falls to 1.58x in 2030 and gradually improves to 3.56x by 2041.

6. **Project Returns:** The model calculates an FCFE IRR of 26.74% and an FCFF IRR of 15.95%.

Model Components:
- Construction Cost and CAPEX Planning
- Debt and Equity Funding Structure
- Interest During Construction (IDC)
- Debt Repayment Schedule
- Depreciation and Tax Calculation
- Projected Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement
- DSCR, IRR and NPV Analysis

Tools & Skills Used:
- Microsoft Excel
- Project Finance Modelling
- Financial Forecasting
- Cash Flow Analysis
- Debt and Equity Structuring
- DSCR, IRR and NPV

Conclusion: The model shows how a large-scale solar project can be planned and evaluated financially. It connects the initial investment and funding requirements with future revenue, operating costs, debt repayment and project returns.
